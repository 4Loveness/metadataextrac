About MetaExtract

MetaExtract is a powerful tool that allows you to extract metadata from various file types without uploading your files to any server. All processing happens directly in your browser, ensuring your data remains private and secure.

Privacy Focused

Your files never leave your computer. All metadata extraction happens locally in your browser.

Multiple Formats

Supports 10+ common file types including documents, images, audio, video, and archives.
